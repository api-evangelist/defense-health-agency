The Military Health System today announced a new digital resource card to support service members who may have experienced a concussive event. The card offers fast and reliable access to trusted brain health tools and services through a convenient, scannable QR code linked directly to the resources page within the MHS Warfighter Brain Health Hub . The digital card is available as a downloadable PDF.
