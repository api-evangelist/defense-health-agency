As the 2026 Military Health System Research Symposium, themed “Harnessing the Power of Military Medical Research,” is approaching, participants can submit their abstracts or nominations for the annual awards. The call for abstracts to submit your research for the Department of War’s premier military medical science meeting closes March 31, 2026, (12 a.m. EST).
