Disease and non-battle injury (DNBI) is a significant threat to military operations, historically exceeding combat injuries in deployed settings. 1-4 Disease and injury (D&I) surveillance supports health risk assessment for the purpose of instituting interventions as needed to promote and maintain the health of deployed forces. 5-7 Defense Health Agency Procedural Instruction (DHA-PI) 6490.03: Deployment Health, effective June 19, 2019, defines standardized surveillance categories for D&I reporting.
