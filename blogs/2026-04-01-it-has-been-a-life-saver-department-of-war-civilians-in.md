The Office of the Assistant Secretary of War for Health Affairs hosted a virtual information session March 10, 2026, to gather feedback on the effectiveness of the Pilot Health Insurance Enhancement for Department of War Civilian Employees In Japan . Launched Jan. 1, 2025, the program provides supplemental health services for approximately 11,000 DOW civilian employees in Japan — addressing challenges when seeking medical care including language barriers and high up-front costs.
