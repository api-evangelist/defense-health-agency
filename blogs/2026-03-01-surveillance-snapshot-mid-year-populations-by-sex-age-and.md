This Surveillance Snapshot describes the mid-year population for active component service members (ACSMs) of the U.S. Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Space Force, and Coast Guard from 2023 to 2025, stratified by age, sex, and race and ethnicity. Population counts were obtained from June of each calendar year using personnel data from the Defense Manpower Data Center (DMDC) maintained in the Defense Medical Surveillance System (DMSS).
