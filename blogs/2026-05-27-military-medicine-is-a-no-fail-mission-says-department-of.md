Military medical experts align efforts, focus on warfighter readiness at annual military health worldwide symposium Top Department of War leaders delivered a unified vision of advancing warfighter readiness and enhancing military health during the opening of the 2026 Military Health System Conference, in Dallas, Texas, May 27. The core of the MHS is the operational readiness and health of the warfighter, said Anthony J. Tata, Department of War undersecretary for personnel and readiness.
