Oropharyngeal cancer develops in the oropharynx, which is comprised of the soft palate, side and back walls of the throat, tonsils, and back of the tongue. 1 Oropharyngeal cancer is distinguished from cancers arising in the oral cavity and pharynx, otherwise known as head and neck cancers, which include the lip, salivary glands, mouth and gums, and entire throat (or pharynx) and tongue. 2 Oropharyngeal cancer comprises 2 distinct cancers, HPV-positive and HPV-negative types, with different risk factors and age distributions.
