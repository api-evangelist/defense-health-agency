Abstract In 2025, the unadjusted incidence rates of heat stroke and heat exhaustion among U.S. active component service members were 39.4 and 170.0 cases per 100,000 person-years, respectively. The rate of heat stroke increased in 2024 and 2025, after declining for 3 years (2021-2023) at the beginning of the 5-year surveillance period.
