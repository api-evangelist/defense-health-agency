Abstract This study characterized chronic insomnia disorder, utilizing data from the Defense Medical Surveillance System, to identify incident cases among U.S. active component service members from 2014 through 2023. A total of 126,733 service members met the case definition and were matched 1-to-1 with service members without chronic insomnia by sex, age, and time in service.
