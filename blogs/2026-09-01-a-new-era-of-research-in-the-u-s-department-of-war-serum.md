Abstract The Department of War Serum Repository (DOWSR, formerly DODSR 1 ) was established in 1989 after the initiation in 1985 of the U.S. Department of War’s universal mandatory human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) screening program. DOWSR, which is currently maintained by the Armed Forces Health Surveillance Division (AFHSD), preserves over 78 million serum specimens.
